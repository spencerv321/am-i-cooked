# Am I Cooked — Improvement Plan, September 2026

Data pulled 2026-09-12 from production `/api/stats/*` (200 days tracked, since 2026-02-25).
Latency/cache numbers from live calls against `claude-sonnet-4-6` with production params.

## State of the app in 5 bullets

1. **Human traffic is ~30 uniques/day and ~9 analyses/day (median 5).** Last 60 days: 1,760 uniques, 547 analyses. The Feb 28 record (2,823) was 300× today's baseline. March averaged 96 analyses/day; June bottomed at 2.2/day; Aug–Sep recovered slightly to 8–19/day. There has been no spike since March — the two best days since June (Sep 7: 84, Sep 2: 58) were a single visitor grinding titles and one `shared-link` burst (24 referrals → 58 analyses).

2. **Page-view analytics are polluted by an unfiltered bot.** Today: 582 page views, 12 uniques, 4 analyses, **95.7% from Brazil**. Lifetime geo is 43.6% BR / 11% CZ. Views-per-unique climbed from 2.3 (April) to 19.8 (September) while uniques stayed flat — that's a crawler with a browser UA, not growth. `direct` = 93.6% of referrers, mostly this. **Any page-view or geo number is currently untrustworthy.**

3. **Share rate is ~1.2% and the sticky CTA converts at 0.45%.** 93 total share clicks across all surfaces against 7,801 analyses. Sticky CTA: 1,984 impressions → 9 shares; 75% auto-dismiss, 11.5% manually dismissed. The core viral loop (score → share → new visitor) is not functioning. What people *do* do is `try_again` (964, 12% of analyses) and browse the leaderboard (510 views, 842 tab switches, 206 job clicks).

4. **Secondary modes are dead or near-dead.** Compare: 21 submits lifetime, 2 shares. Company: 239 analyses (3%), 6 shares, and 49 of those are one person analyzing "astera labs". Trending: 85 impressions since June 11 and 2 clicks — the ≥3 titles × ≥2 analyses/day threshold is almost never met at current volume, so the strip essentially never renders. Email capture: 2 subscribers ever. Personalized description: 15 uses.

5. **Latency is bad and caching is likely net-negative at this volume.** Live test: job analysis 11–12s, company analysis **44s** (1,885 output tokens). Caching *works* (1,652 tokens cached, reads confirmed on 2nd call — the 2,048 minimum in CLAUDE.md is wrong for this model), but the ephemeral TTL is 5 minutes and analyses arrive ~every 2.5 hours. Nearly every production call is a cache *write* (1.25× input price), not a read. Score distribution: avg 51, median 57, Well Done 39.7%, Fully Cooked only 2.2% — 71% of results land in 41–80.

**Anomaly (resolved 2026-09-12 — NOT a scraper):** visitor hash `0d1011…` has 1,499 analyses, all on **Feb 28** — the viral day — and `trust proxy` was only enabled mid-day that day (`cafe012`). Before the fix every visitor arrived as Railway's proxy IP. Same story for the `null` hash (519, Feb 28) and the 100–270-row hashes on Mar 1. These are thousands of real people, the rows are good, and nothing should filter them. Post-June the heaviest real visitor is 88 analyses over three months. The per-IP daily cap added in Session A stays as cheap insurance, but its original justification was wrong.

## Ranked improvements (max 8)

Each item: evidence → target metric → effort → success signal.

### 1. Fix analytics bot filtering (foundation)
- **Evidence:** 95.7% of today's page views from BR with 12 uniques and 4 analyses; views/unique 19.8 vs 2.3 in April.
- **Target:** reliability (of measurement).
- **Effort:** S. Per-IP daily page-view cap (e.g. 30) in `middleware.js`; add ASN-less heuristic: hash with >N views and no `/api/analyze` call gets excluded from `daily_stats`/`geo_stats`/`referrers`. Also add the missing `?ref=` and referrer split to `getReferrerTrend` so `shared-link` bursts are visible.
- **Success:** views/unique drops back under ~4; geo shows US as #1.

### 2. Rebuild the share moment around the thing people actually engage with
- **Evidence:** share rate 1.2%; sticky CTA 0.45% CTR with 75% auto-dismiss (it's noise, not a CTA). But `try_again` is 12% and leaderboard job-clicks are 206 — people want to *compare themselves to others*, not broadcast a number.
- **Target:** share rate.
- **Effort:** M. Concretely: (a) kill the sticky CTA (it has never worked — 9 shares in 1,984 impressions); (b) make the primary result card itself the shareable object — a rendered "receipt"/card image with score, status, percentile, hot take, that the user can save/copy as an image (the OG renderer already exists server-side; expose it as a download/copy-image button); (c) put "vs. [most popular job]" inline on the result — "You're safer than 61% of jobs; plumbers are at 16" — which turns every result into a compare card without the compare mode.
- **Success:** share clicks / analyses > 4% over 30 days (3× current).

### 3. Response cache keyed on normalized job title
- **Evidence:** top 20 titles account for 2,300+ analyses; "plumber" alone is 166. Every one is an 11s, ~$0.01 API call producing a near-identical result. Also solves the latency problem for the most common inputs (cache hit = instant) and is a stronger cost lever than prompt caching.
- **Target:** cost per analysis + latency.
- **Effort:** S–M. `seo_pages` already stores `analysis_json` by slug for 231 titles — reuse it: on `/api/analyze`, if `slugify(title)` exists in `seo_pages` and no description was supplied, return that JSON (still record the analysis row + broadcast). Optional: 10% sampling to regenerate so the leaderboard AVG keeps moving. Only fresh/personalized titles hit Claude.
- **Success:** ≥40% of job analyses served from cache; p50 latency for cached titles < 500ms.

### 4. Fix the leaderboard's data hygiene and make it the front door
- **Evidence:** leaderboard is the most-used secondary surface (510 views, 842 tab switches, 206 clicks → analyses). But it's computed over rows including 49× "astera labs" (company side) and titles like "the abstract concept of air" (score 0, currently #1 least cooked).
- **Target:** return visits + new-visitor growth (leaderboard is the only page that's interesting without typing anything).
- **Effort:** M. ~~(a) Cap per-visitor-hash contribution~~ — **dropped**: the heavy hashes are the pre-trust-proxy viral day, not one user (see anomaly note above). (b) Require a title to appear from ≥3 distinct hashes *on days after 2026-02-28* or ≥3 rows — mainly to keep joke titles ("the abstract concept of air") off the podium; (c) add a "Browse all 231 jobs" grid linking to `/jobs/:slug` pages so the SEO pages get internal links (they currently get 427 views and 9 CTA clicks lifetime — Google isn't sending traffic partly because nothing links to them).
- **Success:** leaderboard job-click → analysis conversion tracked; `/jobs/` page views from `google` referrer up month over month.

### 5. Cut company-mode latency or cut company mode
- **Evidence:** 44s per analysis; 239 uses in 6 months, 3% of traffic, 6 shares, 20% of usage is one person.
- **Target:** cost + reliability.
- **Effort:** S to cut latency (drop `max_tokens` to ~1200, shorten per-dimension `analysis` fields in `companyPrompt.js`, remove `timeline_detail`); L to make it good (V1.5 website scraping from the PRD).
- **Recommendation:** do the S version. Do **not** invest in V1.5 enrichment — the usage doesn't justify it. Keep the mode alive because the `/company/` share URLs and leaderboard exist and it costs nothing idle.
- **Success:** company p50 < 15s.

### 6. Prompt-cache TTL / removal
- **Evidence:** 5m TTL, ~9 calls/day → near-100% miss rate → paying 1.25× on input instead of 0.1×.
- **Target:** cost per analysis.
- **Effort:** S (one line). Switch to `cache_control: { type: 'ephemeral', ttl: '1h' }` — 2× write cost but writes happen once/hour instead of once/call, breakeven at ~2 calls/hour which Sep 2 and Sep 7 exceeded. Or remove `cache_control` and re-add when traffic returns. With #3 in place, this matters less.
- **Success:** `cache_read_input_tokens > 0` on the majority of calls (log `message.usage` in `api.js` — currently not logged anywhere, which is why Q9 needed a live test).

### 7. Score distribution audit with the tool you already have
- **Evidence:** 71% of results in 41–80; Fully Cooked 2.2%. Can't tell from bucket data whether single-score clustering returned post-Sonnet-4.6 (`/api/stats/scores` only buckets). `scripts/analyze-scores.js` (untracked) produces the per-score histogram from the DB.
- **Target:** reliability of the core product claim (differentiated scores).
- **Effort:** S. Run it against the public DATABASE_URL, filter `scoring_version=2 AND created_at > '2026-06-12'`. If any single score >8%, or if ≥81 is structurally unreachable for the data-entry anchor, tune Formula J's `0.95` ceiling / `1.4` exponent in `scoring.js` and bump `scoring_version`.
- **Success:** no single score >8% of post-June rows; data entry clerk anchors ≥88.

### 8. Trending threshold fix (or remove Trending)
- **Evidence:** 85 impressions in 3 months, 2 clicks. `HAVING SUM(count) >= 2` per day + ≥3 titles almost never true at 5–15 analyses/day.
- **Target:** none meaningful — this is cleanup.
- **Effort:** S. Change the window to 7 days and the threshold to ≥2 distinct hashes; or delete the component. Recommend: 7-day window, since it's already built and instrumented.
- **Success:** `trending_impression` on >50% of idle-view loads.
